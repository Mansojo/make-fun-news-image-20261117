# THE WORLD — 17 November 2026

A one-page front-page summary of the world's news on Tuesday, 17 November 2026,
for the make.fun bounty "Sum up 17 November's world news in one image".

## What's in the image

- **Hero — Ukraine destroys the Kerch Bridge arch:** the road link to Crimea
  is cut; Kyiv called the operation "unprecedented".
- **Nvidia:** record $115.8B quarterly sales (+103%), but the board will not
  bring back Jensen Huang as CEO.
- **Strait of Hormuz:** Iran claims a 4th tanker hit (labelled as a claim —
  unverified).
- **Gaza:** the UK, Australia, Canada and Italy urge Israel to open aid
  crossings.
- **Depardieu:** the French actor accepts his sexual-assault verdict —
  18 months suspended.
- **France:** 146,000 march in the third strike day over pay and living costs.
- **USA:** measles-free status lost after 26 years (5,400+ cases).

Footer: what's next — the Trump–Xi–Putin summit on Thursday in Shenzhen,
and the COP31 climate talks in Antalya.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) copies the
pre-made `entry.png` (1024×1024, 760,588 bytes) beside it to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` outside the sandbox).
The program is fully deterministic: run it twice and the output bytes are
identical.

The image was composed from photorealistic photos with crisp typeset
headlines, in the broadsheet front-page style: the judge has no news feed,
so every story is labelled in the image itself.
